Wireless Android Auto AP (HMG-API mod de22ev-wireless-aa 1.1.0) - for DE22EV.KOR V039 260902 only

What it does (at every engine start)
- Makes the AP broadcast this car's own projection SSID/password, the one the car's AA app tells the phone.
  The values are read from each car (ConnectivitySSID/ConnectivityPSK); the package contains none.
  (The hotspot name/password in Settings becomes the same value. The originals are backed up.)
- Turns the wireless projection AP (connectivity mode) on.
- If the boot handshake with the phone was lost (the phone sits on the car's Wi-Fi and Bluetooth
  retrying AA), restarts the car's AA app so it negotiates again (20 s after the AP is up with no
  session, at most twice, never while a session runs).
- Screen (setting "Android Auto 화면 전환"): "button" (default) keeps the car's screen when
  wireless AA connects; the home screen's Android Auto tile opens it. "auto" switches at once, as
  stock. Change it in the HMG-API app's Mods tab and press "Save settings"; it applies from the next
  connection. Wired AA always switches at once.
- At engine off it turns the AP off, and never turns it on while the engine is off.
- Leaves things alone while the car's client Wi-Fi (Wi-Fi tab) or the normal hotspot is on.
Requires: NEMESIS and HMG-API (hmgapid) installed. The mod is signed with its author's own key, so
install.txt carries a "not on HMG-API's trusted author list" warning; the install goes ahead.
It updates an earlier version in place; the daemon restarts on the new version at once, and the
screen hold applies after the next car start.

USB stick
- Unzip and put NEMESIS.data, NEMESIS.sh and the WAA_AA_MOD folder at the top of the USB stick.
- The first line of WAA_AA_MOD/MODE.txt: install (default) / status / uninstall

1) Install or update (MODE.txt = install)
1. Start the car and wait about a minute after boot.
2. Insert the USB stick and choose Yes in Nemesis. It finishes within 30 seconds. Remove the stick.
   STATUS.txt: INSTALLED_RESTART_THE_CAR means it worked. Turn the engine off (until the head unit
   is off) and on again.

2) Check
- After an engine start, wireless AA should connect within about a minute and a half without
  touching the phone, with the car's screen unchanged; the Android Auto tile should open AA; after a
  short engine off/on it should connect again.
- If something is wrong, set MODE.txt to status, run the kit, and keep the WAA_AA_MOD_* folders and
  WAA_AA_MOD_LAST.txt from the stick.

3) Remove (MODE.txt = uninstall)
- Turns the mod off and removes it, restores the original hotspot name/password, turns the AP off
  and sets wifi_ap_on=0. STATUS.txt: UNINSTALLED_RESTART_THE_CAR (the screen hold goes at the next start).

On the car: mod /data/hmgapi/mods/de22ev-wireless-aa, log /data/hmgapi/logs/de22ev-wireless-aa/waad.log
